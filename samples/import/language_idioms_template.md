## Idiom: call it a day

Meaning: stop working for now
Literal translation: chamar isso de um dia
Translation equivalent: encerrar por hoje
Register: casual
Region: UK
Difficulty: 2
Tags: work, daily speech

Example:
After three hours, let's call it a day.

Your sentence:
After debugging, let's call it a day.

Common mistake:
Do not translate it literally.

# Post-MVP Gap Closure Roadmap

The Phase 15 baseline delivers the local StudyFlow MVP. Phases 17 through 25 close broader promises
that were intentionally deferred during MVP narrowing. Later product-completion phases preserve these
contracts while cleaning user-facing truthfulness, offline checkpoint language, interaction honesty,
and documentation.

## Phase 17: Annex file storage and portability

Real annex bytes are copied into app-private storage, verified with SHA-256, cleaned up when orphaned,
and optionally embedded in `.studyplan` packages.

## Phase 18: Material viewer and media playback

Material and video resources become directly usable through safe Android viewer/playback handoff,
app-private FileProvider access, and clear unsupported-source states.

## Phase 19: Practice, test, and mock execution engine

Question banks become answerable attempts with per-question answer records, timers, and summaries.

## Phase 20: Wrong-answer notebook and remediation loop

Incorrect answers feed the error notebook and spaced-review remediation queue.

## Phase 21: Reminders, notifications, and study widgets

Review and schedule reminders use explicit permission handling and configurable notification policy.

## Phase 22: Global search and smart recommendations

Search spans plans, topics, questions, notes, materials, reviews, and errors. Recommendations explain
why a next action is suggested.

## Phase 23: Privacy, encryption, and backup hardening

Exports gain optional encryption, privacy modes, restore dry-runs, and backup integrity reporting.

## Phase 24: Offline checkpoint foundation

The app keeps checkpoint evidence on this device and does not require online identity.

## Phase 25: Gap closure seal

The final gap-closure seal verifies that deferred promises are implemented or explicitly excluded.

# StudyFlow MVP Release Readiness Report

## Covered phases

1. Foundation and Compose app shell
2. Domain model and Room database
3. Complete Study Plan management
4. Portability, snapshots, and warning cleanup
5. Syllabus and study content management
6. Schedule strategy
7. Study sessions and timer execution
8. Question bank and practice
9. Reviews and spaced repetition
10. Library and study resources
11. Goals, notes, and progress
12. Analytics and insights
13. Settings, backup polish, and app shell seal
14. Polish, accessibility, and UX consistency
15. Final integration seal and regression suite

## Release posture

The MVP is feature-complete after Phase 15. Additional work should be treated as post-MVP release
polish, real-device testing, performance tuning, notifications, widgets, or sync.

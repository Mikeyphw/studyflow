# OV16 — Question quota planner

- Uses the active OV14 phase question target; no target means no quota.
- Counts already answered questions from completed sessions and submitted tests/mocks inside the phase.
- Remaining target becomes an inclusive daily pace and exact next-seven-days quota.
- Subject distribution is transparent: 35% exam weight, 20% under-practice, 20% recent accuracy deficit, 15% recurring errors, 10% overdue reviews.
- Unknown evidence contributes zero; no incidence or performance is invented.
- Integer per-subject quotas always sum exactly to the displayed seven-day total.

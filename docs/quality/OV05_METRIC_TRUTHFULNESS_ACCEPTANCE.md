# OV05 Metric Truthfulness Acceptance

OV05 makes Progress metrics distinguish unavailable evidence from an observed zero.

- Zero answered questions render **No data**, never `0%`.
- Once evidence exists, an observed zero remains a legitimate `0%`.
- Accuracy confidence is low below 10 answers, medium from 10 through 49, and high from 50 answers.
- Schedule adherence requires at least one observed/due block before rendering a percentage.
- Goal progress requires at least one recorded goal before rendering a percentage.
- The cleanup-health tool is presented as **Content integrity / Integridade do conteúdo** and retains the existing safe preview/review engine.
- Content integrity is displayed as a score out of 100 rather than as a study-performance percentage.

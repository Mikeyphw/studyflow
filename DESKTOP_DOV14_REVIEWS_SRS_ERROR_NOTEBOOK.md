# Desktop DOV14 — Reviews, SRS, Flashcards, Error Notebook

DOV14 makes retention workflows durable and connected to real practice evidence.

- Reviews, Flashcards and Error Notebook rows persist in `reviews-v1.properties`.
- `ManageStudyReview` remains the source of truth for SRS grading, successor scheduling, skip/suspend and error-remediation policy.
- Wrong answers committed by DOV12 Practice Tests or DOV13 Mock Exams automatically record/recur Error Notebook evidence and ensure a remediation review.
- The Reviews route supports Flashcard creation, grading, skipping, suspension, reclassification and resolution.
- Permanent Plan deletion purges Plan-owned review data.

DOV15 completes this batch with durable local-file/library workflows.
